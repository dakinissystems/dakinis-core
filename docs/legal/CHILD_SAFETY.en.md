# Child Safety Standards — Dakinis Core
Last updated: 2026-05-08

Dakinis Core has a zero-tolerance policy against child sexual exploitation and abuse (CSAE/CSAM).

Any content or behavior related to grooming, exploitation, trafficking, or abuse involving minors is prohibited.

Measures:
- reporting tools
- priority moderation/review
- removal of illegal content
- suspension of infringing accounts
- cooperation with competent authorities

Safety contact: legal@dakinis.local
